Frequently asked questions

Can I exclude just one number from a contact?

Yes. Untick that number while keeping the other numbers selected.

Are my contacts uploaded to a server?

No. This tool reads the selected VCF file locally inside your browser. The site does not send your contacts to a remote server.

Does this change the contacts on my iPhone?

No. Selections affect only the printout and CSV export, not the original file or your iPhone contacts.
